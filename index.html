01 / ABOUT

A mathematical foundation.
A focus on learning.

My work connects the rigour of mathematics with the human questions at the heart of education.

With a background in mathematics teacher education, educational assessment and measurement, and data science, I bring statistical and computational methods to questions about learners’ competencies, assessment design, and learning environments.

My academic experience includes teaching and research at the Faculty of Quality Management, University of Education, Vietnam National University, Hanoi. I completed my Master of Data Science at Deakin University in September 2026.
